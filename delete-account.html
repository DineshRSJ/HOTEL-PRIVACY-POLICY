Delete Your Account and Data

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and data for the app Indian Hotel Tycoon Master.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ratshamediahouse@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my account - Indian Hotel Tycoon Master.
  3. In the email, say whether you want your whole account deleted or only some of your data deleted (for example, your game progress).
  4. We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

When you request account deletion, we delete:

  • Your account login details (email address, name, and account ID)
  • Your saved game progress and game data linked to your account

What may be kept

We do not keep your account information after deletion. Information handled by Google AdMob for advertising is controlled by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Guest players

If you play as a guest, your game data is stored only on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Contact

Questions about deletion? Email ratshamediahouse@gmail.com.
