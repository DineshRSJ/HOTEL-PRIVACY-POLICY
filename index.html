Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Indian Hotel Tycoon Master ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

Guest play: You can play the app as a guest without creating an account. In this case we do not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details, and your game progress is stored on your own device.

Account login: If you choose to log in or create an account, we collect the account details you provide or authorise, such as your email address, name, and a unique account ID. We also store your game progress and related game data so that it can be saved and restored.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device type, and app usage data, to show ads and measure their performance. This is handled by Google under its own policies:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. How We Use Information

We use account information only to provide login, save your game progress, and keep the app working properly. Information collected through advertising services is used only to display ads and measure them. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data Deletion

Guest data is stored on your device and can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. If you have an account and want your account and its stored data deleted, email us at the address below from your registered email, and we will delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
